Background & Philosophy

I'm Raj, a web builder from Chittagong, Bangladesh. I make websites and web tools from scratch in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: no heavy frameworks, nothing I did not shape myself.

My work ranges from study-material sites and an environmental awareness platform to Onyx, my own marketplace with its own AI assistant, Syncro. I care most about how a site looks and feels, so I keep tuning colour, spacing and motion until it fits the project.
